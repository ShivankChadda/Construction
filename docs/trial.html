    How it works

    Nobody has to learn a new app. The group stays the place where work happens. The Purchase Desk is one extra WhatsApp number that only messages the person who has to act.

    Engineers and supervisors

    New: one tap per delivery
    • Keep posting requirements in the group: text, photos, Hindi voice notes.
    • When material is sent, the Purchase Desk lists it. Tap All received, or Short/damaged and type what came.
    • One reminder if a delivery isn't confirmed in 2 days.

    Abbas

    New: forward, then type rates
    • Once or twice a day, select the new posts in the group and forward them together. One summary comes back, sorted by site.
    • Writes rates in his chat with the bot: N1 Om Traders 420.
    • Forwards his dispatch posts too. Gets a to-do list every morning.

    Dad

    New: approves the priced slip
    • Gets each slip with a PDF and alerts: rate up, new vendor, bought recently.
    • Replies the way he does now: haan, Abbas do it, 3 nahi, or a question.
    • His yes issues the slip with his name, stamp and a verification code. A site summary arrives every evening.

    What stops leakage

    Where money leaksWhat the Purchase Desk does
    Approving a need without a priceDad approves the priced slip, so he sees the rate and vendor before saying yes.
    Inflated ratesEvery line is compared with the last approved rate for that item. More than 5% higher is flagged.
    Friendly or new vendorsVendors not on the approved list are flagged.
    Buying twiceFlags the same item bought for the same site in the last 10 days.
    Edited or forged slipsEach approved slip carries a verification code. The QR code opens the original record, so any change shows up.
    Material that never arrivesThe site confirms what came. Shortages go to Abbas and Dad at once.

    What the AI does, and what it never does

    Claude reads messy posts, Hinglish, photos of handwritten lists and Hindi voice notes, and turns them into clean items for the right site. It also matches dispatch lists to approved items.

    Approvals are never decided by AI. Fixed rules read Dad's reply, only his number can approve, and a reply to an old version of a slip does nothing. Voice-note approvals and 👍 reactions always need a confirm tap.
